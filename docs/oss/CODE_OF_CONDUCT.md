# Code of Conduct (Publication Draft)

> This draft describes the intended community standard. It is not adopted until human owners approve
> the public project, enforcement team, reporting channel, and final policy text.

## Our standard

We want participation to be safe, respectful, and technically rigorous. Positive behavior includes:

- showing empathy and patience;
- welcoming questions and different levels of experience;
- criticizing ideas and evidence rather than people;
- acknowledging uncertainty and correcting mistakes;
- respecting privacy, consent, attribution, and boundaries; and
- prioritizing user and operator safety over speed or status.

Unacceptable behavior includes harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized conduct, deliberate
intimidation, sustained disruption, doxxing, publication of private information, bad-faith security
disclosure, and retaliation against a reporter or participant.

## Scope

The policy is intended to apply in project repositories, reviews, issues, discussions, meetings,
events, chat spaces, and public representation of the project. It also applies when conduct outside a
project space creates a credible safety risk for participants.

## Reporting and enforcement

Before publication, human owners must name an enforcement team and configure a confidential reporting
channel that does not require contacting the subject of a report. Public issues are not an acceptable
reporting channel.

The enforcement team should:

1. acknowledge reports privately;
2. identify conflicts of interest and recuse affected reviewers;
3. gather only the information needed for a fair assessment;
4. protect reporter and subject privacy;
5. apply a proportionate, documented response; and
6. provide an appeal path to an uninvolved maintainer or governing body.

Possible responses include a private correction, warning, temporary participation limits, removal of
content or access, and permanent exclusion. Exact enforcement guidelines require human approval before
this draft is activated.

## Attribution decision

Before publication, owners should decide whether to adopt the Contributor Covenant or another standard
policy verbatim. If adopted, retain its required attribution and replace this draft with the approved
text rather than presenting a local adaptation as the upstream policy.
